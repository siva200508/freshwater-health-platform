Urgent Freshwater Threats We Track

Observable physical & chemical contamination indicators cataloged by our platform.

Industrial & Chemical Effluent

Illegal direct discharge of surfactants, heavy metals, and dyes leading to toxic foam formation, chemical odor, and massive fish kills.

Untreated Domestic Sewage

Municipal sewer bypass and drain overflow causing septic stench, extreme coliform bacteria, and severe dissolved oxygen starvation.

Plastic Debris & Solid Waste

Single-use plastics, packaging materials, and discarded styrofoam choking stormwater canals, trapping aquatic waterfowl and degrading into microplastics.

Algal Blooms & Runoff

Excessive nitrate and phosphate agricultural fertilizer wash-off sparking dense microcystin algal carpets and anoxic dead zones.
